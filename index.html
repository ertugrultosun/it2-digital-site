The house rules

Small studio.
Stubborn about details.

  1. i.

    Worlds, not screens.

    Every product gets a place, a light, a few characters and a reason to come back. Screens are just the windows.

  2. ii.

    Tested like we mean it.

    Noisy Neighbors ships with more than 2,150 automated tests, and every one of its levels is proven solvable by two independent solvers.

    The whole building.

    Design, code, sound, store pages, launch. We hold the product from the foundations to the roof, so nothing gets lost on the stairs.
